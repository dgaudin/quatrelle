The spelling dictionaries in this folder are third-party data, not MailCraft code.

- fr_FR.aff.gz, fr_FR.dic.gz — French Hunspell dictionary from Dicollecte
  (https://www.dicollecte.org/), taken unchanged from the hunspell-fr package and
  gzipped. Dicollecte publishes it under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (MPL-2.0),
  the GNU Lesser General Public License and the GNU General Public License; it is
  used here under MPL-2.0, whose text is in MPL-2.0.txt beside this file.
- en_US.aff.gz, en_US.dic.gz — English (United States) Hunspell dictionary from
  SCOWL (http://wordlist.aspell.net/), taken unchanged from the hunspell-en-us
  package and gzipped. Its copyright and licence (MIT-like, BSD-like and public
  domain, at the reader's choice) are those of the SCOWL project.

They are read at run time by the `spellbook` crate (MPL-2.0), also used unchanged.
MailCraft embeds these files in the program (no dictionary is read from the
system), which is why they travel with it on every platform.
